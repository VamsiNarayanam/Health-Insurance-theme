Questions we actually get

Named waits, room rent, porting — answered without a brochure footnote.

These are the six calls the BKC rain desk takes most weeks. If your clause is not here, write cover@stacklycanopy.com.

PED means pre-existing disease. If you declared diabetes, we wait two years on Elder, Kin and Solo unless a rider says otherwise. The clock starts on the policy start date, not the day you first felt unwell. Undeclared PED can void a claim. We would rather you name it on the proposal.

The Kin ₹10L band in this demo has no 1% room-rent cap. Some lower rings do. If a cap exists it is a rupee figure printed on the certificate — not a percentage buried in a brochure. Ask for the specimen before you pay GST.

Portability follows IRDAI rules. Bring the previous policy, NCB letter and proposal at least 45 days before expiry. We do not invent extra waits if the earlier insurer already ran them. A late file can still be a fresh underwrite.
